About ReAlto Partners

What is ReAlto Partners?

ReAlto Partners is a community of real estate agents, co-founded by Dan and Allison Gomer and powered by eXp Realty, the brokerage and parent company. Its mission is to positively impact as many lives as possible through real estate, and its vision is personal and financial freedom for every agent on the team.

Is ReAlto Partners a real estate brokerage?

No. eXp Realty is the brokerage. ReAlto Partners is an organization of agents inside eXp Realty that adds culture, coaching, training and in-person community on top of eXp's cloud-based platform.

Who leads ReAlto Partners?

Dan and Allison Gomer founded ReAlto Partners and lead it together with co-leader Dianne Schell. Dan is a former middle school science teacher and basketball coach, a real estate investor and the host of The Resilient Real Estate Agent podcast. Allison is a Realtor, his wife and his co-host on the podcast.

Where is ReAlto Partners located?

ReAlto Partners was founded in the Denver metro area and has expanded throughout the Front Range. eXp Realty is in all 50 states and more than 30 countries, so we also have agents in Texas, California, Wisconsin and Minnesota. As a cloud-based brokerage, we are happy to talk with agents in all locations.

Do I keep my own brand and business?

Yes. It is your brand and your business, on our platform. You build your own name with your own clients, and ReAlto Partners and eXp Realty give you the tools, training, support and community to grow it.

Do I have to give up my clients or my marketing to join?

No. Your clients, your sphere and your marketing stay yours. We help you do more with them through coaching, systems and the people around you.

Does ReAlto Partners have a podcast?

Yes. The Resilient Real Estate Agent is Dan and Allison Gomer’s video podcast on YouTube. It helps agents beat burnout and build a business that lasts.

How do I join ReAlto Partners?

Fill out the short form on the Why ReAlto page or call Dan at 303.261.5278. The first step is a no-pressure conversation.

Joining the team

Do ReAlto Partners agents ever meet in person?

All the time. Free live events, regular team building events, masterminds, eXp Explained luncheons in Denver and Colorado Springs, and our quarterly Cappers dinner. Cloud based just means we are intentional about it.

How does support work on eXp's virtual platform?

Agents get fast access to managing brokers through eXp World, eXp's virtual campus, plus direct access to everyone in the ReAlto organization: teachers, former managing brokers, team leads, investors and multi-state agents.

How does eXp Realty revenue share work?

eXp Realty shares a portion of company revenue with agents who attract other productive agents to the brokerage. It is paid by eXp, not taken from the other agent's commission. Program rules change over time, so Dan walks through the current plan on a call.

Can I join ReAlto Partners if I am not in Colorado?

Yes. ReAlto is based in the Denver metro, but because eXp Realty is cloud based, agents licensed wherever eXp operates can join and take part in calls, trainings and masterminds online.

I'm not sure what I'd bring to the team.

We believe everyone has a gift to give. If you are unsure what yours is, that is exactly what a first conversation is for.

Does ReAlto only work with residential clients?

No. Most of our agents focus on residential sales, but through eXp Realty you also have access to a luxury brand, a commercial brokerage, a land and ranch division, investment divisions and a referral-only division.

Is a call a commitment?

No. It is a chance to connect and get a feel for who we are. There is no obligation.

eXp Realty

Can agents own eXp stock?

Yes. eXp agents can earn stock through production, milestones and attraction, so many become shareholders in the brokerage they work in.

Do I need to be in Colorado?

No. eXp Realty is in all 50 states and more than 30 countries, and ReAlto Partners already has agents in Texas, California, Wisconsin and Minnesota.

The podcast

What is The Resilient Real Estate Agent?

The Resilient Real Estate Agent is a video podcast on YouTube hosted by Dan Gomer with co-host Allison Gomer.

Who hosts The Resilient Real Estate Agent?

Dan Gomer, co-founder of ReAlto Partners, a group within eXp Realty and a former teacher, with his wife and fellow co-founder Allison Gomer, a Realtor on the team.

Who is the podcast for?

Real estate agents and team leaders who want to beat burnout, grow personally and build a business that lasts, plus anyone curious about the psychology of success.

Where can I watch it?

On YouTube at @theresilientrealestateagent. Subscribe there to get new episodes.

When do new episodes come out?

New episodes air every other Tuesday at 9 a.m. Mountain.
