Meet the Gomers

Partners in business. Partners in life.

ReAlto Partners is led by Dan Gomer, with his wife Allison right beside him as partner and podcast co-host. Together they’re building a team, and a show, around one idea: real estate is a vehicle for changing lives.

Dan Gomer

Teacher. Coach. Investor. Team builder.

Dan is driven by a passion to help people discover an empowering vision for themselves and create the life of their dreams.

Before real estate, Dan spent years as a science teacher and basketball coach. That’s still how he leads: he teaches, he coaches, and he believes in people before they believe in themselves. He went on to invest in real estate, develop businesses and write I’m Full of It and So Are You, a funny, honest book about breaking the stories that keep us stuck.

Today Dan leads ReAlto Partners at eXp Realty, an organization of agent partners built on culture first. His goal is simple to say and big to do: help change 10,000 lives for the better through real estate transactions, wealth building, better agents and a community people want to belong to.

“A better world begins with you.”

Allison Gomer

The heart of the partnership.

Allison is Dan’s wife, business partner and co-host of their new podcast.

Where Dan brings the energy, Allison brings the depth. Her own work centers on intention, connection and the inner side of success: the mindset and wellbeing that make a busy, ambitious life actually feel good to live.

On the show, she’s the one asking the question everyone else was thinking. Together, Dan and Allison talk openly about raising a family while running businesses, leading people, staying connected as a couple and building something that lasts.

What we stand for

Five values. No fine print.

01

Culture is our heartbeat

We attract and keep partners who live our values, creating an environment where everyone can flourish.

02

Service is the path to success

By serving clients, communities and each other, we amplify our individual and collective goals.

03

Resourceful & resilient

We look for solutions instead of worrying about problems, and we share ideas openly. Our collective knowledge is our greatest asset.

04

Integrity in all actions

We highlight each person’s strengths and combine our skills to be strong, ethical people and business owners.

05

Our strength comes from self leadership plus a steadfast commitment to supporting one another. We do what we agree to do.
