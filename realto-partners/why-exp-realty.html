Why eXp Realty · The brokerage behind ReAlto PartnersEarn beyond your own closings.

eXp Realty is a cloud-based, agent-owned brokerage in all 50 states and more than 30 countries. Here is how its model creates recurring income, and how ReAlto Partners helps you use it.

Why eXp Realty

How does eXp Realty create recurring income?

At eXp Realty, agents can earn beyond their own closings. Revenue share rewards agents who help the company grow, and stock programs let agents own part of the brokerage. That is how ReAlto Partners builds income together, and why every new partner strengthens the whole pack.

01

Revenue share

When agents you bring to eXp close transactions, eXp can pay you a share of company revenue. It comes from eXp, not from their commission.

02

Agent ownership

eXp agents can earn company stock through production, milestones and attraction, so you can own a piece of the brokerage you work in.

03

Cloud freedom

No desk fees and no office walls. Brokers, training and support are reachable from anywhere, and ReAlto adds the in-person community most cloud brokerages miss.

Revenue share, stock awards, splits and caps are programs of eXp Realty and change from time to time. Ask Dan for the current details. Income is never guaranteed and depends on individual effort.

Questions about eXp Realty

Is ReAlto Partners a real estate brokerage?

No. eXp Realty is the brokerage and parent company. ReAlto Partners is a group of agents inside eXp Realty that adds culture, coaching, training and in-person community.

How does eXp Realty revenue share work?

eXp Realty shares a portion of company revenue with agents who attract other productive agents to the brokerage. It is paid by eXp, not taken from the other agent's commission. Program rules change over time, so Dan or Dianne will walk you through the current details.

Can agents own eXp stock?

Yes. eXp agents can earn stock through production, milestones and attraction, so many become shareholders in the brokerage they work in.

Do I need to be in Colorado?

No. eXp Realty is in all 50 states and more than 30 countries, and ReAlto Partners already has agents in Texas, California, Wisconsin and Minnesota.
