About Allison

Who is Allison Gomer?

Allison Gomer is a Colorado Realtor and partner at ReAlto Partners at eXp Realty. She helps buyers and sellers across the Denver metro and co-hosts The Resilient Real Estate Agent podcast with her husband, Dan Gomer.

Allison has spent her life helping people through big transitions, and a move is one of the biggest. She listens first, keeps people steady when the stakes are high and helps families get clear on what they actually want from their next home.

Inside ReAlto Partners she is Dan’s partner on the people side of the business. She cares about how agents feel, not just what they produce, and she is always nudging the team toward slowing down, being intentional and celebrating each other.

On The Resilient Real Estate Agent she brings the human side of the 3R Framework: what resilience looks like at the kitchen table, not just on the stage.
