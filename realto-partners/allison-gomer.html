About Allison

Who is Allison Gomer?

Allison Gomer is a Colorado Realtor and partner at ReAlto Partners at eXp Realty. A former psychotherapist, she helps buyers and sellers across the Denver metro and co-hosts The Resilient Real Estate Agent podcast with her husband, Dan Gomer.

Allison spent years as a psychotherapist before joining Dan in real estate. That background shapes everything she does with clients: she listens first, keeps people calm when the stakes are high, and helps families make clear decisions about where and how they want to live.

Inside ReAlto Partners she is Dan’s partner in building the team’s culture, with a focus on the people side of the business: connection, wellbeing and helping agents stay resilient for the long run.

On The Resilient Real Estate Agent, she brings the inner work behind outer success, the part of the 3R Framework that is easy to talk about and hard to practice.

Beyond real estate

Cacao ceremonies and guided meditation

Alongside real estate, Allison still guides cacao ceremonies and meditation through her own practice, allisongomer.com. A cacao ceremony is a guided ritual centered on drinking ceremonial cacao with intention, paired with breathwork and meditation.

  1. Sacred space

    The room and an altar are prepared so you can arrive and settle.

  2. Intention

    You set a clear intention for what you want to release, invite or understand.

  3. Cacao

    Warm ceremonial cacao, taken slowly and mindfully.

  4. Breath & meditation

    Breathwork leads into a guided meditation journey.

  5. Integration

    A closing circle to share, reflect and carry the insight forward.

Ceremonies run 60 to 90 minutes and are offered privately, for two people, for small groups of up to ten and for teams. They make a meaningful reset for agents, couples and companies.

Questions about Allison Gomer

Who is Allison Gomer?

Allison Gomer is a Colorado Realtor with ReAlto Partners at eXp Realty, the wife and business partner of team leader Dan Gomer, and co-host of The Resilient Real Estate Agent podcast on YouTube. Before real estate she worked as a psychotherapist.

Can Allison help me buy or sell a home in Colorado?

Yes. Allison helps buyers and sellers across the Denver metro area, including Highlands Ranch and Douglas County. Reach her through the contact page or call 303.261.5278.

How does Allison's psychotherapy background help her clients?

Buying or selling a home is one of the most stressful things people do. Allison's years as a therapist taught her to listen closely, stay calm under pressure and help people make clear decisions, which is exactly what a move requires.

What is a cacao ceremony?

A cacao ceremony is a guided ritual built around drinking ceremonial cacao, a minimally processed form of the cacao bean, with intention. Allison pairs it with breathwork and guided meditation so it becomes a calm, heart-centered space to set intentions and reflect.

How long does one of Allison's cacao ceremonies last?

Typically 60 to 90 minutes. Private and group ceremonies include an intention call beforehand. Booking is through allisongomer.com.

Is ceremonial cacao safe?

Ceremonial cacao is food, not a psychedelic. It contains theobromine and a small amount of caffeine, which are mild stimulants. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, or take antidepressants or other medications, check with your doctor before attending.
