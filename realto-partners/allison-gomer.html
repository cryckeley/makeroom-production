About Allison

Who is Allison Gomer?

Allison Gomer is a Colorado psychotherapist who moved beyond traditional talk therapy to guide people through meditation, mindfulness and ceremony, including cacao ceremonies for individuals, couples, groups and teams.

Allison has practiced meditation and ceremony for about ten years. She first met cacao at a day-long meditation retreat in early 2018, and it quickly became part of her daily meditation and her monthly ceremonial practice.

Today she weaves together the tools she trusts most: talk therapy, body-based somatic meditation, chakra and energy work meditations, archetypal images and metaphor, breathwork and ceremony. Every ceremony and meditation is crafted for the person or group in front of her.

Allison has shared her approach on podcasts including Science and Spirituality, where she talked about the science of cacao, how to prepare it and what a ceremony involves. Now she brings that same depth to the new Dan & Allison Gomer podcast.

Cacao ceremony

What happens in a cacao ceremony with Allison?

A cacao ceremony is a guided ritual centered on drinking ceremonial cacao with intention. Allison pairs cacao with breathwork and meditation to deepen the experience. Here is the flow.

  1. Sacred space

    Allison prepares the room and an altar so you can arrive and settle.

  2. Intention

    You set a clear intention for what you want to release, invite or understand.

  3. Cacao

    You drink warm ceremonial cacao slowly and mindfully.

  4. Breath & meditation

    Breathwork leads into a guided meditation journey written for you.

  5. Integration

    A closing circle to share, reflect and carry the insight forward.

Cacao ceremony questions

What is a cacao ceremony?

A cacao ceremony is a guided ritual built around drinking ceremonial cacao, a minimally processed form of the cacao bean, with intention. Allison pairs it with breathwork and guided meditation so the experience becomes a calm, heart-centered space to set intentions and reflect.

What happens during one of Allison's cacao ceremonies?

Allison prepares a sacred space and altar, guides you in setting an intention, serves the cacao, leads breathwork and a guided meditation, and closes with a circle for sharing and integration.

How long does a cacao ceremony last?

Allison's ceremonies typically last between 60 and 90 minutes. Private and group ceremonies include an intention call beforehand, and individual and duo ceremonies include a follow-up session.

Who are the ceremonies for?

Anyone looking for a reset: individuals, couples, friends, groups of up to ten and corporate teams. No meditation experience is needed. Each ceremony is crafted for the person or group in the room.

Where does Allison offer ceremonies?

Ceremonies are held in person in the Denver metro area of Colorado. Her nine-session Sacred Psychotherapy and Ceremony program combines in-person sessions with Zoom. Book through allisongomer.com.

Is ceremonial cacao safe?

Ceremonial cacao is food, not a psychedelic. It does contain theobromine and a small amount of caffeine, which are mild stimulants. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, or take antidepressants or other medications, check with your doctor before attending.
