Dan Gomer · Co-founder of ReAlto Partners · eXp Realty member, ColoradoTeacher. Coach. Investor. Team builder.

Dan Gomer leads ReAlto Partners, a community of real estate agents at eXp Realty based in the Denver metro area. He believes real estate is the best vehicle he knows for changing lives, starting with the agents on his team.

Biography

Who is Dan Gomer?

Dan Gomer is a Colorado real estate team leader, investor and speaker who co-founded ReAlto Partners, a group within eXp Realty, with his wife, Allison, to help agents build personal and financial freedom.

Before real estate, Dan spent years as a middle school science teacher and basketball coach. That is still how he leads: he teaches, he coaches, and he believes in people before they believe in themselves.

He got his start flipping houses in Denver, then turned his attention to helping people buy and sell homes. After about a decade at Your Castle Real Estate, he moved to eXp Realty in 2022 and began building ReAlto Partners, a team defined by culture first.

Today Dan also hosts The Resilient Real Estate Agent on YouTube with his wife and partner, Allison Gomer, a Realtor on the team.

His goal is simple to say and big to do: help change 10,000 lives for the better through real estate, by serving clients, helping families build wealth, making other agents better and bringing people into a community they want to belong to.

“A better world begins with you.”
