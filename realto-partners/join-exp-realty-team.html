Who we’re looking for

What kind of agent thrives at ReAlto Partners?

Experience level matters less than character. New agents, seasoned producers and team leaders all fit, if they share these traits.

  • You honor your commitments. You do what you say you’ll do.
  • You look for solutions in tough times. You move forward instead of stalling.
  • You are driven to improve yourself. Personal growth isn’t optional.
  • You see the power of community. You participate, give and receive.
  • You want to help grow a strong culture.
  • You are positive, resilient and ready to grow.
  • You regularly show up. Camera on, ready to listen, learn and lead.
  • You value connection. We love connecting with great people who see what a strong community brings to a successful business and, more importantly, to a strong life.

Our values

The 4 R’s that hold us together.

Resourcefulness, Resiliency, Responsibility and Reliability are the basis of our value system. They are how we choose partners, how we show up for each other and how we protect our culture as we grow.

R1

Resourcefulness

We are proactive problem solvers. We look for solutions on our own while asking for help when we need it, and we put the help we get to work.

R2

Resiliency

Markets change and setbacks happen. We stay positive, keep a growth mindset and keep moving forward, together.

R3

Responsibility

We own our success and our actions. It is easier, more enjoyable and more rewarding to build it alongside like-minded people.

R4

Reliability

We do what we agree to do, to the best of our ability, no excuses. We show up on time, ready to listen, learn and lead.

The eXp advantage

What do agents get at eXp Realty with ReAlto Partners?

eXp Realty is a cloud-based, agent-owned brokerage. ReAlto Partners is the community that makes it feel like home. Together, here is what that gives you.

01

Revenue share

Help grow eXp by attracting great agents and earn a share of company revenue on their closed transactions, paid by eXp.

02

Agent equity

Earn eXp stock through production, milestones and growth. Many agents become shareholders in their own brokerage.

03

Competitive splits

A straightforward split with an annual cap, so more of what you earn stays with you as you grow.

04

Brokers on demand

Managing brokers and support available through eXp World, without waiting for office hours.

05

Training everywhere

eXp’s daily classes plus ReAlto’s own: lead generation, tech, open houses, goal setting and personal growth.

06

The ReAlto layer

Teachers, former managing brokers, team leads, investors and multi-state agents. Whatever you’re stuck on, someone has solved it.

Revenue share, stock programs, splits and caps belong to eXp Realty and are subject to change. Dan will walk you through current details on a call. Earnings are not guaranteed and depend on individual effort and results.

Questions agents ask before joining

Do ReAlto Partners agents ever meet in person?

All the time. Breakfasts, happy hours, quarterly charity events, masterminds, eXp Explained luncheons, an annual camping trip and a golf tournament with a traveling trophy. Cloud based just means we are intentional about it.

How does support work on eXp's virtual platform?

Agents get fast access to managing brokers through eXp World, eXp's virtual campus, plus direct access to everyone in the ReAlto organization: teachers, former managing brokers, team leads, investors and multi-state agents.

How does eXp Realty revenue share work?

eXp Realty shares a portion of company revenue with agents who attract other productive agents to the brokerage. It is paid by eXp, not taken from the other agent's commission. Program rules change over time, so Dan walks through the current plan on a call.

Can I join ReAlto Partners if I am not in Colorado?

Yes. ReAlto is based in the Denver metro, but because eXp Realty is cloud based, agents licensed wherever eXp operates can join and take part in calls, trainings and masterminds online.

I'm not sure what I'd bring to the team.

We believe everyone has a gift to give. If you are unsure what yours is, that is exactly what a first conversation is for.

Is a call a commitment?

No. It is a chance to connect and get a feel for who we are. There is no obligation.
