The 3R Framework

What is the 3R Framework?

The 3R Framework is the foundation of The Resilient Real Estate Agent: three habits that keep agents growing through any market. It grows out of the same values that run ReAlto Partners.

R1

Resiliency

Bouncing back from lost deals, slow markets and burnout, and building the mental stamina to play the long game.

R2

Resourcefulness

Finding a way when the market shifts: creative solutions, the right people and tools, and the courage to ask for help.

R3

Responsibility

Owning your results, doing what you agree to do, and leading yourself before you lead anyone else.

Podcast questions

What is The Resilient Real Estate Agent?

The Resilient Real Estate Agent is a video podcast on YouTube hosted by Dan Gomer with co-host Allison Gomer. It is built on the 3R Framework of resiliency, resourcefulness and responsibility.

Who hosts The Resilient Real Estate Agent?

Dan Gomer, co-founder of ReAlto Partners at eXp Realty, author and former teacher, with his wife and fellow co-founder Allison Gomer, a Realtor on the team.

Who is the podcast for?

Real estate agents and team leaders who want to beat burnout, grow personally and build a business that lasts, plus anyone curious about the psychology of success.
