Why ReAlto

We love people.

If our culture and values resonate with you, we want to connect. We’re looking for agents who see real estate as a way to build a great living and to make a real difference in thousands of lives.

Start the conversation How eXp works

Who we’re looking for

Good people who want to get better.

Experience level matters less than character. You might be a fit if you:

  • Honor your commitments. You do what you say you’ll do.
  • Look for solutions in tough times. You move forward instead of stalling out.
  • Are driven to improve yourself. Personal growth isn’t optional for you.
  • See the power of community. You participate, give and receive.
  • Want to help grow a strong culture. You’d rather build it than just benefit from it.
  • Are positive, resilient and ready to grow.
  • Regularly show up. Camera on, ready to listen, learn and lead.

The eXp advantage

Your closings pay you. Your team can too.

eXp Realty is a cloud-based, agent-owned brokerage. ReAlto Partners is the community that makes it feel like home. Here’s what that combination gives you.

01

Revenue share

Help grow eXp by attracting great agents and earn a share of company revenue on their closed transactions, paid by eXp, not out of their commission.

02

Agent equity

Earn eXp stock through production, milestones and growth. Many agents become shareholders in the brokerage they work with.

03

Competitive splits

A straightforward split with an annual cap, so more of what you earn stays with you as your business grows.

04

Brokers on demand

Managing brokers and support available through eXp World, eXp’s virtual campus, without waiting for office hours.

05

Training everywhere

eXp’s daily classes plus ReAlto’s own: lead gen, tech, open houses, goal setting and personal growth.

06

The ReAlto layer

Teachers, former managing brokers, team leads, investors and multi-state agents. Whatever you’re stuck on, someone’s solved it.

Revenue share, stock programs, splits and caps belong to eXp Realty and are subject to change. Dan will walk you through the current numbers on a call. Earnings are not guaranteed and depend on individual effort and results.

Honest answers

The questions everyone asks.

Do you ever actually see each other in person?

All the time. Breakfasts, happy hours, quarterly charity events, masterminds, an annual camping trip and a golf tournament with a traveling trophy. Cloud based just means we get to be intentional about it.

How does support work on a virtual platform?

You get nearly immediate access to managing brokers through eXp World, plus every agent in our organization. The answer you need is usually one message away.

I’m not sure what I’d bring to the team.

We believe everyone has a gift to give. If you’re unsure what yours is, that’s exactly what a first conversation is for. We’d love to learn about your strengths and goals.

Is a call a commitment?

No. It’s just a chance to connect and get a feel for who we are. There’s only one way to know if this is all just nice words, and that’s to talk to us.
