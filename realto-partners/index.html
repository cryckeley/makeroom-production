Meet Dan

From the classroom to a movement of agents.

Dan started as a middle school science teacher and basketball coach. Years of real estate investing, a self help book and a lot of hard-won lessons later, he leads a growing organization of agent partners at eXp Realty.

His author voice is the same one you get on a Wednesday call: funny, direct and convinced that you already have what you need to build the life you want. I’m Full of It and So Are You is about breaking the stories that hold us back. ReAlto Partners is where he puts that into practice with real agents and real businesses.

Why eXp

A brokerage that pays you to build something bigger.

ReAlto Partners lives inside eXp Realty, a cloud-based brokerage where agents can earn beyond their own closings. That’s how Dan’s team creates recurring income together, and why every new partner strengthens the whole pack.

01

Revenue share

When agents you attract to eXp close deals, you can earn a share of company revenue. It isn’t taken from their pocket. It’s eXp’s model for rewarding the people who grow it.

02

Ownership

eXp agents can earn stock in the company through production, milestones and attraction. You’re not just working at a brokerage, you can own a piece of it.

03

Cloud freedom

No desk fees and no office walls. Brokers, training and support are reachable from anywhere, and ReAlto adds the in-person community that cloud brokerages usually miss.

Revenue share, stock awards, splits and caps are programs of eXp Realty and change from time to time. Ask Dan for the current details. Income is never guaranteed and depends on individual effort.
