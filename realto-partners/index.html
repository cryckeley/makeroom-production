Why eXp Realty

How does an eXp Realty team create recurring income?

At eXp Realty, agents can earn beyond their own closings. Revenue share rewards agents who help the company grow, and stock programs let agents own part of the brokerage. That is how Dan’s team builds income together, and why every new partner strengthens the whole pack.

01

Revenue share

When agents you bring to eXp close transactions, eXp can pay you a share of company revenue. It comes from eXp, not from their commission.

02

Agent ownership

eXp agents can earn company stock through production, milestones and attraction, so you can own a piece of the brokerage you work in.

03

Cloud freedom

No desk fees and no office walls. Brokers, training and support are reachable from anywhere, and ReAlto adds the in-person community most cloud brokerages miss.

Revenue share, stock awards, splits and caps are programs of eXp Realty and change from time to time. Ask Dan for the current details. Income is never guaranteed and depends on individual effort.

Questions about ReAlto Partners

What is ReAlto Partners?

ReAlto Partners is a community of real estate agents, co-founded by Dan and Allison Gomer and powered by eXp Realty, the brokerage and parent company. Its mission is to positively impact as many lives as possible through real estate, and its vision is personal and financial freedom for every agent on the team.

Is ReAlto Partners a real estate brokerage?

No. eXp Realty is the brokerage. ReAlto Partners is an organization of agents inside eXp Realty that adds culture, coaching, training and in-person community on top of eXp's cloud-based platform.

Who leads ReAlto Partners?

Dan and Allison Gomer founded ReAlto Partners and lead it together with co-leader Dianne Schell. Dan is a former middle school science teacher and basketball coach, a real estate investor and the host of The Resilient Real Estate Agent podcast. Allison is a Realtor, his wife and his co-host on the podcast.

Where is ReAlto Partners located?

ReAlto Partners is based in Highlands Ranch in the Denver metro area, with team members in Colorado, Texas, California and Wisconsin. Because eXp Realty is cloud based, we are happy to talk with agents from anywhere.

Does ReAlto Partners have a podcast?

Yes. The Resilient Real Estate Agent is Dan and Allison Gomer’s video podcast on YouTube. It helps agents beat burnout and build a business that lasts.
